# Privacy Policy — ScaleCalc and ScaleCalc Pro

Responsible individual or company: Eduardo Ferreira. Contact: caducodeapps@gmail.com. Last updated: 13/11/2026.

## Scenario data

Values entered into the calculators are processed on your device and saved locally so that you can continue working with the same scenario across screens and when you reopen the app. The developer does not receive these values through a developer-operated server. This data may be included in Android backups, depending on your device settings. When exporting a scenario, you choose where to save the file. If you choose a cloud service, that service's terms also apply. Importing a scenario replaces your inputs after confirmation.

## ScaleCalc Free: advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent choices where applicable. The advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address (including to estimate your approximate location), product interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Its behavior depends on the applicable settings and choices. Do not state that the free app collects no data simply because calculator values remain on the device.

When required by the SDK, you can review your advertising choices under **More → Advertising privacy options**. This option appears when UMP indicates that it is required.

Information about Google's practices: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Advertising Technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## ScaleCalc Pro: paid version

Pro does not include AdMob, UMP, or advertising. The purchase required to download the app is processed by Google Play; the app does not request payment card details. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Payment information held by Google is subject to Google's policies.

## Managing your data

You can clear local app data in your Android settings or uninstall the app. Exported files must be deleted from the location you selected. Backups and payment information associated with your Google Account have their own management controls.

## Intended audience and policy changes

The app is a tool for capacity estimation and system architecture. Recommended for software engineers and architects. App updates may require changes to this policy. For questions or requests, contact: caducodeapps@gmail.com.
